Advanced Visualizations

Heat Map and Parallel Coordinates - multi-dimensional insights

Technique 1

Heat Map: Age x Diagnosis Readmission Risk

Why selected: A heat map is ideal for visualizing a 2D matrix where color encodes readmission risk across age and diagnosis simultaneously, enabling pattern detection across 90+ combinations at once.
Insights revealed: Patients aged 20-40 with Diabetes as primary diagnosis show anomalously high readmission rates. Circulatory conditions drive high readmission across all age groups. Injury shows lower readmission in older patients.
vs Traditional chart: A bar chart would require 9 separate charts to show the same data. The heat map compresses all comparisons into one scannable view.

30-Day Readmission Rate Heatmap

Age Group x Primary Diagnosis - darker = higher risk

Technique 2

Parallel Coordinates: Multi-Variable Patient Profile

Why selected: Parallel coordinates reveal correlations across multiple continuous variables simultaneously - stay duration, medications, lab procedures, diagnoses count - impossible to see in a 2D chart.
Insights revealed: High-readmission diagnosis groups cluster with longer stays and more medications. Low-readmission groups show shorter stays with fewer interventions, revealing a clear resource-risk relationship.
vs Traditional chart: Scatter plots show only 2 variables at once. A parallel coordinates plot with 5 axes replaces 10 separate scatter plots while preserving inter-variable structure.

Each line = diagnosis category; colored by readmission rate
